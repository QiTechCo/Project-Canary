Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Our commitment

A campaign website should be usable by every voter, including people who browse with a screen reader, navigate by keyboard, need larger text, or prefer reduced motion. We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

What we have done

  • Every page starts with a “Skip to main content” link and uses a single, correctly ordered heading outline.
  • All content is reachable and operable with a keyboard alone, including the photo gallery and its full-screen viewer.
  • Every interactive element has a visible focus indicator.
  • Text and interface colours meet or exceed a 4.5:1 contrast ratio.
  • All images carry alternative text; decorative images are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Animation and motion effects are switched off automatically for anyone whose device requests reduced motion.
  • Form fields have visible labels, helpful error messages, and correct autocomplete hints.
  • The site works at 200% zoom and reflows to a single column on small screens.

Known gaps

We would rather tell you what is not done yet than claim otherwise:

  • Captions for the campaign video clips are being produced and are not yet published.
  • The volunteer portal has not yet had a full screen reader audit.

Tell us if something does not work

If any part of this site gets in your way, please tell us — we will fix it and, where we can, get you the information you were after another way. Contact details are on the homepage. We aim to respond within two business days.
