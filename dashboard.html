Field Readiness & Guides

Canvassing Training Hub

Field-tested scripts, door-to-door best practices, and downloadable training materials for Team Dimple volunteers.

1. The 60-Second Script
Introduction & Connection

"Hi, my name is [Name], and I'm a volunteer with Council Member Dimple Ajmera's campaign. We're out in [Neighborhood] talking to neighbors about tree canopy protections, clean water, and affordable housing..."

Pro-Tip: Ask open-ended questions like "What is the top issue you'd like City Council to focus on?"
2. Core Talking Points
Environment & Fiscal Record
  • Environment: Leading the fight for clean water and pausing unregulated data centers.
  • CPA Leadership: Budget committee chair protecting taxpayer dollars without cutting city services.
  • Public Safety: Supported by first responders and FOP Lodge 9.
Pro-Tip: Never guess! If you don't know an answer, note their email and say the campaign team will follow up.
3. Field Safety & Turf
Best Practices on the Doors
  • Always canvas in pairs or check in with your shift captain.
  • Respect "No Soliciting" or gated warnings.
  • Step back 3-4 feet from the door after knocking.
  • Log responses immediately in the app after each house.
Pro-Tip: Wear comfortable shoes, stay hydrated, and wear your Dimple campaign t-shirt or pin.
