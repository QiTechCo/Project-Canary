# Empowering Charlotte’s Small Businesses: The Path to Growth and Opportunity

**Published:** Fri, 07 Aug 2026 02:00:59 GMT  
**Direct Link:** [https://dimpleajmera.substack.com/p/empowering-charlottes-small-businesses](https://dimpleajmera.substack.com/p/empowering-charlottes-small-businesses)

---

Our small businesses are the heartbeat of our communities and the driving force behind a resilient local economy. As a city, we are deeply committed to ensuring that our entrepreneurs are fully equipped to take advantage of the incredible opportunities ahead and help build Charlotte’s future.

Eligibility for these opportunities begins with certification.

With $25.5 billion in voter-approved transit and roadway projects slated over the next 30 years across Mecklenburg County, now is the ideal time for local small businesses to position themselves to compete through **Small Business Enterprise (SBE) Certification**.

### Why Get Certified?
The city’s small business certification program provides a direct pathway to city contracting opportunities while opening doors to vital networking, educational resources, and direct support designed to help businesses grow and compete.

Getting certified means having a seat at the table. For business owners ready to take the next step, certification offers tangible results.

Take it from Daren Daye of DMD Supply Services Group, a certified SBE who has experienced the value of certification firsthand:

> “A lot of our growth has to do with the position CBI puts us in to network with primes and first-tier subcontractors. Through those relationships, we have been awarded repeat business and have been on numerous CMS projects. CBI is a tool that small business owners cannot afford to not utilize, it’s a pathway to success.”
### Take Action Today
Daren’s story highlights the true significance of SBE certification and the exciting trajectory awaiting small businesses as we look toward major transit and roadway projects ahead.

Could your business benefit from certification? The answer is yes. The program connects eligible businesses with contracting opportunities, valuable business resources, and crucial networking events.

If you own a small business—or know someone who does—I invite you to explore the [Small Business Certification page](https://www.google.com/search?q=cltgov.me/certified) to apply and get certified today. Let’s build Charlotte’s future together.
