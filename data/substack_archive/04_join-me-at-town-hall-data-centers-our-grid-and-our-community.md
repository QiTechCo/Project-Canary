When we talk about the future of Charlotte, we’re talking about balance.

​Our city is growing rapidly, and with that growth comes modern digital infrastructure. Data centers keep our connected world running, but they also bring critical questions that hit close to home: *How will this impact our power grid? What does it mean for our local water supply? And how do we ensure our neighborhoods truly benefit from this development?*

​These aren't just technical questions—they are community decisions. That’s why I want to hear directly from you.

​Join me for a community Town Hall focused specifically on data center growth, energy reliability, and local resource management.

- ​**When:** Thursday, August 20 | 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM- ​**Where:** Project 658 (*3646 Central Ave*)- ​**RSVP:** Space at Project 658 is limited, so please **[[reserve your spot here](https://cltgov.me/ajmera)]** ahead of time.
​Whether you're concerned about utility rates, neighborhood environmental impacts, or long-term policy, your perspective is vital. I’ll be joined by key community leaders and policy experts so we can have an open, honest conversation about where Charlotte goes from here.

​Bring your questions, bring a neighbor, and let’s shape our city’s policy together.
