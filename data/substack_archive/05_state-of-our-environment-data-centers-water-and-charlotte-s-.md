# State of Our Environment: Data Centers, Water, and Charlotte's Future

**Published:** Fri, 24 Jul 2026 18:05:55 GMT  
**Direct Link:** [https://dimpleajmera.substack.com/p/state-of-our-environment-data-centers](https://dimpleajmera.substack.com/p/state-of-our-environment-data-centers)

---

As our city experiences rapid technological and population growth, the choices we make today will echo across generations. That is why At-Large City Council Member Dimple Ajmera is inviting residents to an essential community conversation addressing the intersection of our natural resources, local infrastructure, and the massive surge in tech development.

Following the City Council’s unanimous vote for a 150-day moratorium on new data center petitions—a crucial step championed to establish responsible guardrails around energy and water consumption—this town hall brings together local experts and community members to look ahead at Charlotte’s environmental future.

### Event Details

- **Date & Time:** August 20th, 2026, from 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM- **Location:** Project 658, 3646 Central Ave., Charlotte, NC 28205- **RSVP:** Seats are limited and registration is strictly required.
The decisions shaping our public health, clean water, and power grids depend on active community engagement. Make sure your voice is heard in defining the policies that come next.

**Secure your spot:** Use the [official registration portal link on cltgov.me/ajmera](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://cltgov.me/ajmera) to reserve your seat before capacity is reached.
