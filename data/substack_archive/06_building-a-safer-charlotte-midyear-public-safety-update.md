# Building a Safer Charlotte: Midyear Public Safety Update

**Published:** Thu, 16 Jul 2026 21:23:26 GMT  
**Direct Link:** [https://dimpleajmera.substack.com/p/building-a-safer-charlotte-midyear](https://dimpleajmera.substack.com/p/building-a-safer-charlotte-midyear)

---

The Charlotte-Mecklenburg Police Department (CMPD) released its midyear public safety report yesterday, and the data paints a clear picture: our collaborative, proactive approach to public safety is working. **Key Highlights:**

- **Overall Crime is Down:** We’ve seen a 16% reduction in overall crime during the second quarter of 2026.- **Violent Crime Reduction:** Violent crime has decreased by 8%, and property crime is down by 17%.- **Specific Improvements:** We are seeing significant double-digit declines in key areas, including robberies (-23%), larcenies from auto (-29%), vehicle thefts (-31%), and burglaries (-33%).
While these numbers are encouraging, our work continues. Chief Patterson noted a slight increase in homicides, with a 75% clearance rate, and emphasized that community cooperation remains our most powerful tool for solving violent crime.<br><br>**Investing in Our Youth **A central focus of this briefing was our commitment to youth safety. By coordinating across the Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools Police, Parks & Recreation, and the Office of Youth Opportunity, we are maintaining a vital safety net for our teens.

While juvenile-suspect crimes are down 15%, incidents of “teen takeovers” remain a priority that demands our urgent attention. 

Public safety is a shared responsibility. As we move through the second half of the year, I encourage you to stay engaged, register your security systems with ConnectCharlotte.org, and report suspicious activity to CMPD or anonymously through [P3 Tips Mobile App](http://charlottecrimestoppers.com/sitemenu.aspx?P=P3App&ID=372).

Thank you to our officers, community partners, and every resident who helps make Charlotte a safer place to live, work, and thrive.
