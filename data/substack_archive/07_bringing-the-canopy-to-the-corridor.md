# Bringing the Canopy to the Corridor

**Published:** Tue, 14 Jul 2026 13:32:14 GMT  
**Direct Link:** [https://dimpleajmera.substack.com/p/bringing-the-canopy-to-the-corridor](https://dimpleajmera.substack.com/p/bringing-the-canopy-to-the-corridor)

---

As we navigate the peak of summer here in Charlotte, we are reminded of just how critical our natural environment is to our daily lives. Growing and maintaining a healthy tree canopy isn’t just about making our city look beautiful—it is one of our most effective, proven strategies for heat mitigation and community wellness.

That is why the City of Charlotte has a bold goal: we are working to plant 800 trees every year, aiming for **50% total canopy coverage by 2050**.

But to make this vision a reality, we need to ensure that environmental investments are distributed equitably.

### Prioritizing Equity in Our Neighborhoods
For too long, our historic tree canopy has been unequal. Many of our neighborhoods, particularly along our **Corridors of Opportunity**, have not traditionally enjoyed the same rich, shaded spaces as other parts of the city.

As your At-Large Councilmember, I am fully committed to changing that. We are intentionally prioritizing our city planting strategies to bridge this gap and equalize the canopy across *every* zip code. Trees do heavy lifting for our neighborhoods: they provide vital shade, cool down local surface temperatures, and directly help lower energy costs for homeowners.

### How You Can Help (For Free!)
Our Landscape Management team works tirelessly, but they cannot be everywhere at once. We need your local eyes and neighborhood expertise to spot where new growth is needed most.

The city has officially launched an easy-to-use [request portal](https://www.charlottenc.gov/City-Government/Departments/General-Services/Tree-Management/Request-Street-Tree) where any resident can request a free tree planting on city property.

- **Where do they go?** Typically in the city right-of-way, which is the space between the sidewalk and the street in front of your home or business.- **How does it work?** You submit the request, and our municipal arborists handle the rest. They will assess the space to determine the perfect species for the location—ensuring small maturing trees go under power lines and larger trees thrive in open spaces.- **What does it cost?** Absolutely nothing. It is a completely free investment in your property and our community.
If there is a right-of-way in front of your home where you feel a tree could flourish, please take two minutes to submit a request. Let’s work together to keep the Queen City green, cool, and equitable for generations to come.
