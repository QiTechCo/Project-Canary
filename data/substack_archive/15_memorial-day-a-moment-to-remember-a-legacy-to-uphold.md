# Memorial Day: A Moment to Remember, A Legacy to Uphold

**Published:** Mon, 25 May 2026 12:03:42 GMT  
**Direct Link:** [https://dimpleajmera.substack.com/p/memorial-day-a-moment-to-remember](https://dimpleajmera.substack.com/p/memorial-day-a-moment-to-remember)

---

> Originally established as Decoration Day in the wake of the Civil War, Memorial Day stands as a solemn reminder of the true cost of our freedom. The holiday’s deep roots trace back to leaders like U.S. Representative John A. Logan, a staunch defender of the Union, who championed a late-May observance because he believed the tribute should occur when flowers across the country were in full bloom. What began as a tradition to decorate the graves of Civil War soldiers eventually evolved, and in 1971, Congress declared Memorial Day a national holiday to honor every American service member who made the ultimate sacrifice in service to our nation.

As we gather with family and friends this weekend, it is vital that we carry this history forward. In 2000, Congress and the President signed the National Moment of Remembrance Act into law to ensure that the core meaning of the day is never lost in the long weekend. It encourages all of us to step away from our celebrations, if only for a brief moment, to focus on the collective debt of gratitude we owe to the fallen.

Please join me this Monday at 3:00 PM local time, wherever you happen to be. Let’s pause together for one minute of silence to reflect on the profound sacrifices made for our benefit, our community, and future generations. Have a safe, meaningful, and reflective Memorial Day.
