# Why I Proposed these Budget Adjustments for Charlotte

**Published:** Wed, 20 May 2026 00:44:16 GMT  
**Direct Link:** [https://dimpleajmera.substack.com/p/why-i-proposed-these-budget-adjustments](https://dimpleajmera.substack.com/p/why-i-proposed-these-budget-adjustments)

---

At this week’s budget adjustment meeting, I advocated for several targeted changes because I believe we can invest in public safety and community wellbeing while also respecting the affordability pressures families are facing.<br><br>Here’s what I pushed for:<br><br>**Supporting Safe Alliance**<br>I supported restoring funding for Safe Alliance because protecting survivors of domestic violence and sexual assault is public safety work.<br><br>Safe Alliance provides critical services including emergency support, legal advocacy, protective-order assistance, and a 24/7 hotline. These services help prevent violence from escalating and support some of our most vulnerable residents.<br><br>**Standing Up for Firefighters**<br>I also advocated for stronger support for our firefighters, including addressing pay parity concerns.<br><br>Firefighters respond to emergencies every day. If we say public safety is a priority, our compensation structure should reflect that commitment.<br><br>I also support exploring modified-duty positions for firefighters injured in the line of duty so experienced personnel can continue serving the city while recovering.<br><br>**Reducing the Property Tax Burden**<br>At the same time, I asked the City Manager to explore options to reduce the proposed property tax increase while preserving core investments.<br><br>Families across Charlotte are already managing rising housing costs, utilities, insurance, and everyday expenses. Government should continuously look for efficiencies, phasing strategies, and smarter prioritization before asking residents to pay more.<br><br>I listened to local discussions this week about how county leaders balanced investments while holding the tax rate steady. They may have “Magic Mike,” but maybe Charlotte can find a little “Magic Marcus” too.<br><br>That’s not about avoiding investment. It’s about balancing growth with affordability and making sure we are responsible stewards of taxpayer dollars.<br><br>Charlotte can continue investing in safety, mobility, and opportunity while also remaining affordable for working families, seniors, and small businesses.<br><br>Thank you for staying engaged in this process.
