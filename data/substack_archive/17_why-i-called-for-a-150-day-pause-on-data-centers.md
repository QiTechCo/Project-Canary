# Why I Called for a 150-Day Pause on Data Centers

**Published:** Tue, 12 May 2026 13:50:18 GMT  
**Direct Link:** [https://dimpleajmera.substack.com/p/why-i-called-for-a-150-day-pause](https://dimpleajmera.substack.com/p/why-i-called-for-a-150-day-pause)

---

Last night, Charlotte City Council unanimously approved moving forward with a public hearing and potential 150-day moratorium on new data center petitions after I called for a temporary pause so our city can put clear guardrails in place before more projects move forward.

This is not about stopping growth. It is about leading responsibly.

Right now, residents across Charlotte are raising legitimate concerns about water usage, noise, backup generators, energy demand, and neighborhood impacts — especially as our city faces worsening drought conditions.

One proposed facility in northeast Charlotte is projected to use 1.2 million gallons of reclaimed water per day during peak summer months.

I also spoke with a resident raising a child with autism near one of these proposed sites who fears the constant 24/7 humming noise from cooling systems and generators.

That stayed with me.

Because this conversation is no longer theoretical. It is about health, quality of life, and whether families can peacefully live in their homes.

A 150-day pause gives Charlotte time to study the impacts, engage the public, and establish thoughtful standards before additional projects move forward under rules that may no longer match the scale of this industry.

Leadership means addressing challenges before they become crises — and that is exactly why I called for this pause.
