# Happy Mother’s Day

**Published:** Sun, 10 May 2026 19:12:59 GMT  
**Direct Link:** [https://dimpleajmera.substack.com/p/happy-mothers-day](https://dimpleajmera.substack.com/p/happy-mothers-day)

---

My mom immigrated to this country with a language barrier, little certainty, and a dream for a better future for her children.

She left behind everything familiar and worked long hours and taking on odd jobs so my siblings and I could have opportunities she never had. I watched her stretch every dollar, sacrifice endlessly, and still somehow lead our family with love, dignity, and hope.<br><br>As a little girl, I didn’t fully understand the weight she carried.<br>As a mother of two young daughters today, I understand it completely.<br><br>I now understand the quiet sacrifices.<br>The sleepless nights.<br>The constant balancing act.<br>The determination to build a better future for your children while still showing up for your community.<br><br>My mom taught me something powerful:<br>Women do not have to choose between being devoted mothers and strong leaders.<br><br>In fact, motherhood has made me a better public servant.<br>It has made me more compassionate, more resilient, more disciplined..<br><br>One of the most emotional parts of this journey has been watching my mom stand at polling locations for hours — rain or shine — believing in me every step of the way. Her sacrifices became the foundation for everything I’ve been able to accomplish.<br><br>To my mom: thank you for showing me what courage looks like.<br>And to every mother balancing family, work, service, and dreams of your own — you should never be underestimated.<br><br>Happy Mother’s Day. 🌸💖
