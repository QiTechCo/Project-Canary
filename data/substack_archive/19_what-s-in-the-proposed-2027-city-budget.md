# What’s in the Proposed 2027 City Budget?

**Published:** Tue, 05 May 2026 21:04:01 GMT  
**Direct Link:** [https://dimpleajmera.substack.com/p/whats-in-the-proposed-2027-city-budget](https://dimpleajmera.substack.com/p/whats-in-the-proposed-2027-city-budget)

---

Yesterday, the City Manager presented the proposed FY2027 budget — a plan that outlines how the city may invest in safety, housing, transportation, and infrastructure in the year ahead.<br><br>Here’s a breakdown of the highlights that could impact residents.

The proposed budget focuses on:<br>- Public safety investments<br>- Transportation and infrastructure upgrades<br>- Affordable housing and community investment<br>- Continued planning for a rapidly growing city<br><br>It also includes proposed changes to property taxes and service fees, which will be considered during the budget process.

### Public Safety Investments
A major portion of the proposal focuses on strengthening our emergency response and ensuring the safety of our rapidly expanding footprint. The goal is to decisively improve response times and support our first responders.

- **10% pay increase** for police personnel and a **7% pay increase** for fire personnel.- A new fire engine (Engine 46) to improve response times in critical coverage areas.- A $10M investment in a new police helicopter hangar.- Additional police vehicles to expand capacity across the city.- $4M for upgraded fire equipment.
### Transportation & Mobility
Significant funding is aimed at how we move throughout the city. The focus here is on creating safer streets, reducing congestion, and committing to long-term infrastructure planning.

- Proposed **$300M Transportation & Neighborhood Bonds**.- Increased investment in road resurfacing and traffic signal upgrades.- Expanded bicycle infrastructure and the launch of the Orphan Roads program.- Acceleration of our long-term “Big Moves 2030” projects.
### Affordable Housing & Community Investment
Addressing our growth means maintaining accessibility for all residents. The budget includes vital investments tied to housing and economic mobility:

- Continued prioritization of affordable housing initiatives.- Strong support for workforce development programs.- Targeted investments tied to housing access and long-term affordability.
### Proposed Cost Changes
To support these initiatives, the proposal includes adjustments that will be heavily discussed and debated during the upcoming budget process:

- A proposed property tax increase.- Modest increases to storm water and water/sewer fees.
### What Happens Next?
The budget process is just beginning, and your input is a vital part of how these decisions are finalized. Here is the timeline:

- **May 11:** Public Hearing- **May 18:** Budget Adjustments- **June 1:** Straw Votes- **June 8:** Final Vote
