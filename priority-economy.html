Fiscal Integrity & Empowering Charlotte's Entrepreneurs

As a Certified Public Accountant with corporate financial management experience at Deloitte and TIAA, Dimple Ajmera brings unmatched financial oversight to the Charlotte City Council, ensuring tax dollars are spent efficiently and transparently.

1. Empowering Small Businesses through SBE Certification

As highlighted in Dimple's official Substack dispatch (Empowering Charlotte’s Small Businesses: The Path to Growth and Opportunity), Charlotte has over $25.5 billion in voter-approved transit and roadway investments slated over the next 30 years. Dimple is actively helping local business owners get Small Business Enterprise (SBE) certified to win city prime contracts and subcontracts.

Why SBE Certification Matters:

"Our small businesses are the heartbeat of our communities and the driving force behind a resilient local economy. The city’s certification program connects entrepreneurs with vital networking, educational resources, and direct support to compete and win."

2. CPA Budget Oversight & Tax Relief Focus

Dimple scrutinizes the multi-billion dollar city budget line-by-line. She proposed targeted budget adjustments to eliminate waste, safeguard vital public services, and ease financial burdens on working families grappling with inflation.

3. Revitalizing Historic Business Corridors

Through the city's Corridors of Opportunity initiative, Dimple directs infrastructure upgrades, façade improvement grants, and business incubation into historically under-resourced commercial areas across East and West Charlotte.

4. Workforce Training & Next-Gen Jobs

Partnering with Central Piedmont Community College (CPCC), local trade unions, and tech employers, Dimple champions paid apprenticeships and STEM workforce pipelines for Charlotte high school and college graduates.
