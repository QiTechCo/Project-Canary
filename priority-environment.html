Protecting Our Natural Resources for the Next Generation

As Charlotte faces unprecedented population growth and industrial expansion, Council Member Dimple Ajmera has been the city's foremost voice fighting to protect our drinking water reservoirs, air quality, and tree canopy.

1. Leading the 150-Day Data Center Moratorium

When tech developers proposed massive data centers near Charlotte residential neighborhoods and water basins, Dimple sounded the alarm. She called for a mandatory 150-day pause on new data center petitions to evaluate energy grid loads, noise pollution, and massive municipal water consumption. Her motion passed City Council with unanimous support.

The Core Question Dimple Raised on Substack:

"While Charlotte residents and small businesses are asked to practice water conservation during drought conditions, large data centers must operate under strict, transparent environmental standards. We cannot compromise our clean water security for speculative sprawl."

2. The State of Our Environment Town Hall

To ensure the public is directly involved in policy creation, Dimple organized the August 20, 2026 Town Hall: State of Our Environment: Data Centers, Water & Charlotte's Future at Project 658, bringing together hydrologists, energy analysts, conservationists, and neighborhood leaders.

3. Bringing the Canopy to the Corridor

Through the "Trees for Charlotte" initiative and community partnerships, Dimple has championed aggressive tree planting in historic underserved corridors like Sugar Creek and West Boulevard, combating urban heat islands and improving air quality for working families.

4. Clean Energy & Transit Decarbonization

Winner of the 2019 Blue Sky Award from Clean Air Carolina, Dimple voted to transition Charlotte's municipal fleet and CATS bus network toward zero-emission electric vehicles, backed by clean solar investments on city-owned facilities.
