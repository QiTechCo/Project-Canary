Building Communities Where Everyone Has a Place to Call Home

As Charlotte continues to experience rapid economic growth, the cost of housing has created severe cost-burdens for hardworking working families. Dimple Ajmera combines her CPA financial acumen with progressive housing policy to maximize every municipal dollar invested.

1. Passing and Overseeing M+ Housing Trust Fund Bonds

Dimple consistently champions Charlotte's Housing Trust Fund bonds, leveraging city bond revenue with private and philanthropic capital to build, preserve, and rehabilitate thousands of affordable housing units across all council districts.

2. Expanding First-Time Homebuyer Down-Payment Assistance

Homeownership is the primary engine of generational wealth in America. Dimple fought to increase funding for the HouseCharlotte Down Payment Assistance Program, helping first-time buyers with up to ,000 in assistance for down payments and closing costs.

3. Anti-Displacement & Legacy Senior Protections

Rising property valuations in gentrifying corridors should not price long-term elderly residents out of the homes they built. Dimple supports targeted property tax relief grants and aging-in-place home repair funds that keep legacy residents in their neighborhoods.

4. Holding Major Developers Accountable

As reported in her Substack policy briefs, Dimple holds major commercial hospital and biotech developments (including The Pearl innovation district) accountable to their binding affordable housing and workforce hiring pledges.
