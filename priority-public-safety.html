A Proven Champion for First Responders & Community Safety

Public safety is the foundational responsibility of municipal government. On City Council, Dimple Ajmera has led landmark legislative efforts that put officers, their families, and Charlotte residents first.

1. Historic Healthcare Coverage for Fallen Officer Families

Following the tragic death of CMPD Officer Joshua Eyer and fellow law enforcement officers killed in the line of duty during the April 2024 shootout, Council Member Dimple Ajmera took immediate action. She championed policy change to provide permanent city-paid healthcare benefits for the families and children of fallen CMPD officers.

"Councilwoman Ajmera championed providing city healthcare coverages for families of employees killed in the line of duty after the death of CMPD Officer Joshua Eyer... Due to Councilwoman Ajmera, this benefit was unanimously approved by all members of council."

— Charlotte-Mecklenburg Fraternal Order of Police (FOP Lodge 9)

3. Reclaiming High-Crime Corridors

Dimple actively partners with local, state, and federal law enforcement to target violent crime hotspots. Her advocacy helped lead the coordinated joint operation dismantling illicit hubs along the Sugar Creek corridor, restoring safety and investment into long-neglected business districts.

4. Safe Streets & Pedestrian Infrastructure

Safety also means protecting our children, pedestrians, and cyclists. Dimple continues to secure funding for red-light camera enforcement, enhanced pedestrian crosswalks, street lighting in high-risk transit corridors, and traffic-calming measures near Charlotte schools.
