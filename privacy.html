How we handle your information

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Note for the campaign: this policy describes what the website actually does today, based on its code. Have counsel review it against North Carolina campaign finance and privacy requirements before launch, and delete this box once they sign off.

The short version

We collect the contact details you choose to give us, and we use them to organise volunteers. We do not sell your information. We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

What we collect

When you fill in the volunteer form, we ask for:

  • Your name — so organisers know who they are talking to.
  • Your email address — to contact you about shifts, and as your sign-in for the volunteer portal.
  • Your phone number (optional) — for day-of updates when a shift changes.
  • How you would like to help — canvassing, phone banking, hosting, translation, driving, and similar.

If you go on to use the volunteer portal, we also store the district you select, your general availability, the shifts you sign up for, and the hours you log.

What we do not collect

  • We do not run Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any other advertising or analytics tracker on this site.
  • We do not use cookies for tracking. The volunteer portal stores a sign-in token in your browser so you stay logged in; that is all.
  • We never ask for, and never store, payment card details. Contributions are handled entirely by ActBlue under their privacy policy.

How we use it

Volunteer information is used to coordinate campaign activity: assigning shifts, sending event reminders, and following up after events. Campaign staff and authorised volunteer coordinators can see it. It is not shared with, sold to, or rented to anyone else.

North Carolina campaign finance law requires the campaign to publicly report certain information about contributors. That requirement applies to donations, not to volunteering, and is handled through ActBlue and the campaign’s filings — not through this website.

Where it is stored

Volunteer records are held in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database in the United States. Access is restricted by row-level security: a signed-in volunteer can read and change their own record and nobody else’s, and visitors who are not signed in cannot read volunteer records at all.

How long we keep it

We keep volunteer records for the duration of the campaign and for as long afterwards as record-keeping rules require. You can ask us to delete your record sooner.

Your choices

  • See what we hold. Sign in to the volunteer portal, or email us and we will tell you.
  • Correct it. Update your details in the portal at any time.
  • Delete it. Email us and we will remove your record.
  • Stop hearing from us. Every campaign email has an unsubscribe link, and you can reply STOP to any text message.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Volunteers under 18 are welcome at many campaign events with a parent or guardian’s consent — contact the campaign office to arrange it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and note the change here.

Contact us

Questions about this policy, or a request about your information, can go to the Committee to Elect Dimple Ajmera. Contact details are on the homepage.
