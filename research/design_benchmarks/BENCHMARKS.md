# Design Intelligence & High-Conversion Benchmark Database

This database catalogs architectural, typographic, layout, and visual UX benchmarks from the highest-performing political campaign, civic tech, SaaS, and editorial web platforms.

## Core Benchmark Categories

1. **Tier-1 Political & Civic Landing Pages**
   - Hero split-view typography hierarchy
   - Candidate cutout integration & background ambient shading
   - Grassroots engagement pathways (Volunteer Hub, Shift RSVP, Donation Funnels)
   - Trust and endorsement badge grids (verified monochromatic & full-color logo treatments)

2. **Modern Typography & Editorial Layouts**
   - High-contrast serif headlines paired with crisp geometric sans body
   - Fluid typography scales (`clamp()`) and optimal line lengths (`65-75ch`)
   - Editorial pull quotes, policy deep-dive readability, and Substack integration

3. **Motion, Polish & Micro-Interactions**
   - Subtle scroll reveals, backdrop blurs, glassmorphism badges
   - Interactive modal lightboxes with focus trap and keyboard navigation
   - Accessible WCAG 2.2 AA contrast ratios and focus rings

4. **Component Systems & Design Tokens**
   - Color palettes: Navy/Midnight base, Patriot Crimson action, Emerald Civic accent, Warm Gold accents
   - Button hierarchies: Primary Crimson gradient, Emerald Green event buttons, Substack Orange pills, Secondary frosted ghost buttons
   - Clean responsive grid alignment with zero horizontal scroll bleed
