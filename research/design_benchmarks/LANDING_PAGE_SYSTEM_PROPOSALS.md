# Design Proposals: 4 Interconnected Landing Pages & Mobile Field PWA

This document outlines the 4 unified landing page archetypes designed for **Dimple Ajmera for Charlotte** and how they interface directly with the **Mobile Field Volunteer PWA**.

---

## 1. Landing Page Concepts (4 Archetypes)

### Concept 1: The High-Conversion Flagship Split Hero
- **Visual Focus**: Balanced split hero combining Dimple's retina portrait cutout with high-impact serif typography (*"Working Mother • Accountant • Fighter for Charlotte"*).
- **Core CTAs**: Dual conversion path: Patriot Crimson **Donate** (ActBlue) and Emerald Green **Town Hall RSVP**.
- **Social Proof Layer**: Floating stats counter bar (*4-Term Councilwoman*, *100% Tree Canopy Protection*, *18+ Organizational Endorsements*).
- **Role**: Primary homepage and general public entry point.

### Concept 2: The Midnight Editorial & Policy Briefing
- **Visual Focus**: Deep navy / midnight background (`#0f2042`) with luminous emerald green and gold accent metrics.
- **Core CTAs**: **Join Campaign** and **Read Policy Briefs**.
- **Component System**: Modular multi-photo grid showcasing real town hall discussions, clean water forums, and community listening sessions.
- **Role**: Policy deep-dives, environmental action, and press/media releases.

### Concept 3: The Scandinavian Minimalist Action Bento Grid
- **Visual Focus**: Clean off-white canvas (`#fbfbfb`) with expansive whitespace and four distinct modular Bento cards.
- **Core Pillars**:
  1. *Sustainable Tree Canopy & Clean Water*
  2. *Affordable Housing & Transit Corridors*
  3. *Community Safety & First Responders*
  4. *Economic Mobility for Small Businesses*
- **Role**: Issue-focused voter education and quick platform scannability.

### Concept 4: The Grassroots Mobilization & Volunteer Hub
- **Visual Focus**: Energetic community banner (*"Join Team Dimple on the Ground"*), interactive shift registration cards with live capacity meters, and real-time impact counters (*Doors Knocked, Hours Logged*).
- **Core CTAs**: 1-click shift signups and downloadable media toolkits.
- **Role**: Converting supporters into active field volunteers.

---

## 2. The Mobile Field Progressive Web App (PWA)

### Interconnection with Desktop Pages:
- When volunteers sign up on **Concept 4 (Volunteer Hub)**, they receive an instant prompt to **"Add to Home Screen"**.
- Once saved, the app launches in fullscreen standalone mode (no browser URL bar).

### Mobile Field Tool Features:
1. **Live Canvassing Dashboard**:
   - Turf assignment card (*e.g., East Charlotte District 5*).
   - Instant action buttons: **Start Door-to-Door Canvass**, **Open Script**, and **Navigate in Maps** (deep link to Apple/Google Maps).
2. **Offline Policy Search**:
   - Instant search across Substack dispatches and candidate talking points even without cell service.
3. **Ergonomic Bottom Thumb-Bar**:
   - Fixed tabs at screen bottom: **Shifts**, **Wiki**, **Media**, **Profile**.
