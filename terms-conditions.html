Privacy Policy & Mobile Terms of Service

Our commitment to protecting your personal data, privacy rights, and maintaining strict transparency standards across the Committee to Elect Dimple Ajmera.

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

The Committee to Elect Dimple Ajmera is committed to protecting your online privacy. We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information, and SMS opt-in data will never be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Data Protection Standards

Privacy Policy

This policy describes our obligations and practices of how we collect, store, and utilize supporter information, volunteer profiles, and opt-ins for our website, grassroots volunteer hub, email dispatches, and texting services. This Privacy Policy applies to all digital platforms operated by The Committee to Elect Dimple Ajmera.

Categories of Information Collected

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you sign up to volunteer, attend a town hall, subscribe to candidate updates, make a contribution, or opt in to SMS communications. This may include:

  • Full Name and Contact Details (Email address, phone number, mailing address)
  • Charlotte Neighborhood, District, or Zip Code
  • Volunteer Interests, Availability, and Shift Registrations
  • Indication of explicit consent to receive mobile text messages (SMS and MMS)
  • Donation transaction details processed securely through ActBlue (we do not store credit card numbers)

How We Use the Information Collected

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Coordinate grassroots volunteer activities, canvassing schedules, and event logistics.
  • Confirm explicit consent and deliver subscribed campaign updates, Substack dispatches, and community town hall notices.
  • Send critical voter information, early voting dates, polling location reminders, and election deadlines.
  • Comply with North Carolina State Board of Elections and municipal campaign reporting regulations.

Sharing & Third-Party Protections

Strict No-Sale & No-Spam Policy:

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will never be shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information is only shared under the following limited conditions:

  • With vetted vendors, database hosts (Supabase), and service providers working strictly on behalf of the campaign under confidentiality agreements.
  • If disclosure is strictly required by applicable North Carolina or Federal campaign finance laws, court orders, or law enforcement regulations.

Policy Updates & Contact

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in campaign practices or applicable election laws. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

SMS & Messaging Terms

Mobile Terms of Service

The Committee to Elect Dimple Ajmera’s mobile messaging campaigns provide subscribers with official campaign announcements, town hall invitations, volunteer opportunities, donation appeals, and vital nonpartisan voting reminders.

How to Opt-Out

You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Simply text STOP to any message received. After sending STOP, you will receive a confirmation message and no further SMS messages will be sent.

How to Opt-In Again

If you wish to rejoin the messaging program after opting out, sign up via our volunteer or subscribe forms at dimpleajmera.com/volunteer.

How to Get Help

If you experience issues with the messaging program, reply with the keyword HELP for assistance, or contact our campaign team directly at Dimple@DimpleAjmera.com.

Message Frequency & Rates

Message frequency varies; you can expect to receive no more than 2 text messages per day. Message and data rates may apply. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Questions Regarding Our Privacy Standards?

Our campaign compliance team is available to assist you with any questions.
