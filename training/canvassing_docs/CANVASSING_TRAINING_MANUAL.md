# Team Dimple: Canvassing & Field Training Manual

Welcome to **Team Dimple**! Grassroots door-to-door canvassing is the single most effective way to engage voters, share Council Member Dimple Ajmera's record, and get out the vote across Charlotte.

---

## 1. The Core 60-Second Door Knock Flow

1. **Step Back & Smile**: After knocking or ringing the bell, take 3-4 steps back to give the resident space.
2. **Warm Introduction**:
   > *"Hi, my name is [Your Name], and I'm a neighborhood volunteer with Council Member Dimple Ajmera's campaign. We're out in [Neighborhood] talking to voters about priorities for Charlotte City Council. Do you have a quick minute?"*
3. **Listen & Connect**:
   > *"What are the most important issues facing you and your family right now—such as affordable housing, clean drinking water, pedestrian safety, or property taxes?"*
4. **Share Dimple's Record**:
   - **Environment & Water Security**: Dimple is a proven leader fighting to protect Charlotte's tree canopy and ensuring data centers don't overburden our water supply.
   - **Fiscal Discipline (CPA)**: As Budget Committee Chair, Dimple manages city finances responsibly without compromising essential community services.
   - **Public Safety**: Backed by first responders and the Fraternal Order of Police (FOP Lodge 9).
5. **Ask for Support & Voting Plan**:
   > *"Can Dimple count on your vote in the upcoming municipal election?"*
6. **Log the Response**:
   - Record response in the mobile app immediately after walking to the next sidewalk.

---

## 2. Handling FAQs on the Doors

- **Q: Which district is Dimple running for?**
  - **A**: Dimple represents the entire city as an **At-Large City Council Member**, meaning every registered Charlotte voter can vote for her on their ballot!
- **Q: Where can I learn more?**
  - **A**: Direct them to [dimpleajmera.com](https://www.dimpleajmera.com) and hand them a campaign palm card.

---

## 3. Field Safety Rules

- Never enter a resident's home. Stay on the porch or walkway.
- Respect gated properties, aggressive pets, and "No Soliciting" signs.
- Stay hydrated and canvass with a partner.
